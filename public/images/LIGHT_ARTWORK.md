# Light-mode artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP quality 84.
Original night assets are unchanged. Both daylight images are 1536 × 1024.

## hero-day-drive.webp — final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape background illustration for a light-mode driving education app home hero, NOT a UI mockup. Primary request: bright clear morning drive toward a distant Tashkent-inspired skyline with slender TV tower. A modern teal sedan seen from rear three-quarter on the RIGHT third, driving on a clean gently curving road. Premium polished semi-realistic digital illustration. Pale blue sky, fresh emerald trees, silver-gray asphalt, soft warm ivory sunlight. Wide 1536x1024 landscape composition. Left half quiet pale ivory atmospheric negative space for dark UI text; most detail and car on right. Optimistic natural daylight, crisp car, no night lighting, no neon, no murky haze. No text, letters, logos, watermark, UI elements or border.

## daily-road-day.webp — final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape background illustration for a light-mode daily driving challenge card, NOT a UI mockup. Primary request: a beautiful winding gray road through fresh green mountain foothills and pine trees on a sunny spring morning, a red-and-white inverted triangular yield sign clearly visible on the RIGHT side of the road. Premium polished semi-realistic digital illustration. Bright mint green, emerald, warm ivory, pale sky blue, gentle golden sunlight. Wide 1536x1024 landscape composition, road and sign concentrated in right half, left half quiet pale ivory and light sage atmosphere for dark UI text. Crisp inviting scene, no dark vignette, no neon, no night colors. No text, logos, watermark, UI elements or border.

## Icons

The light SVG variants are deterministic palette adaptations of the existing
vector set, not generated raster icons. Deep emerald, blue, violet, and amber
replace neon strokes. They are included in ../pravai-icons.zip under light/.
